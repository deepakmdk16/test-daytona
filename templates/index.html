About Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is a second-degree polynomial equation in a single variable. The discriminant (Δ = b² - 4ac) determines the nature of the roots:

  • Δ > 0: Two distinct real roots
  • Δ = 0: One repeated real root
  • Δ < 0: Two complex conjugate roots
